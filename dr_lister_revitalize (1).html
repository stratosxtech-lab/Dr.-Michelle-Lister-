About Dr. Lister

A cardiologist who listens
as closely as she diagnoses.

Dr. Michelle Lister is a board-certified cardiologist with Revitalize Care MD, serving patients at our Aventura location. She brings dedicated cardiovascular expertise to every encounter — combining evidence-based medicine with the kind of attentive, compassionate care that puts patients first.

Dr. Lister evaluates and manages a broad range of cardiovascular conditions, from coronary artery disease and heart failure to hypertension and arrhythmia. Her approach emphasizes thorough diagnostic workup and clear communication so patients understand their condition and feel confident in their path forward.

As part of the Revitalize Care MD team, Dr. Lister works alongside a network of interventional and vascular specialists — giving patients access to comprehensive cardiac and vein care under one trusted roof.

What Dr. Lister Treats

Conditions & Services

Comprehensive cardiac evaluation and management for patients who need answers and ongoing care they can count on.

Coronary Artery Disease

Evaluation and long-term management of blockages in the arteries supplying the heart, reducing risk of heart attack and protecting function.

Heart Failure

Diagnosis and ongoing management of the heart's reduced ability to pump, with treatment plans designed to improve quality of life and reduce hospitalizations.

Hypertension

Personalized blood pressure management, including advanced evaluation for resistant hypertension and tailored medication protocols.

Atrial Fibrillation

Rhythm management and stroke risk reduction strategies for patients with AFib, coordinated with the full Revitalize Care MD cardiology team.

Echocardiogram

Noninvasive ultrasound imaging of the heart to assess chamber size, valve function, and pumping strength — often the first study for symptoms or a murmur.

Stress Testing

Supervised exercise or pharmacologic testing that evaluates how the heart responds under demand — used to detect coronary artery disease and guide treatment.

Preventive Cardiology

Evidence-based risk stratification and cardiovascular risk reduction — blood pressure, lipids, lifestyle — before disease develops.

Women's Cardiology

Cardiovascular care that accounts for how heart disease presents differently in women, through pregnancy, menopause, and beyond.
